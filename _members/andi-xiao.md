Andi is an undergraduate at New York University studying Neuroscience and Data Science. Her current research focuses on the trade-off between associative recognition and item-level memory fidelity during encoding and retrieval. She is also interested in the computational principles that govern the collaboration between schema activation and episodic memory. She hopes to continue bridging neural and normative approaches to understanding how schema and episodic memory systems collaborate. Outside the lab, she enjoys making multi-media artworks and playing Tetris.
